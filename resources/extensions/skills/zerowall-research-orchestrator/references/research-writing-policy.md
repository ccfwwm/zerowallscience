# ZeroWall Research writing policy 7.0.0-1

Use this route only when the user is writing or reviewing the output of an active ZeroWall ResearchStudy. Ordinary editing, standalone literature reviews and independent manuscript work keep their requested workflow. Determine the active study with `research_study` and actual session context; do not create a clinical study merely to format a document.

For an active study, this policy replaces upstream ARS stage-by-stage confirmation, context-reset, external-client path and default-off claim-audit rules:

1. Read the persistent question, dataset contracts, current plan, tasks, evidence and claims. Pass their IDs and this policy to each writing/review role. A Material Passport may describe writing progress but cannot replace ResearchStore.
2. Gate 1/2 are read-only diagnostics. They always allow the workflow to proceed and may return warnings; they do not approve, freeze, or create prerequisites. Runs retain the Study document versions and hashes used at submission. Proceed through authorized analysis, drafting, reference checking and formatting without asking the user for audit materials or routine confirmation. A user's explicit request to run or continue authorizes that submission in the current turn. Actual permission, schema, parameter, data-contract and runtime errors remain actionable failures.
3. Core-claim audit remains useful for assessing claim support, but it is not a prerequisite for analysis or formal report generation. Load `zerowall-claim-audit` when claim-level verification is requested or materially relevant; retain supported, contradicted, unknown and stale evidence distinctly. A `final` report may be generated with unaudited claims or pending evidence review, provided the report and manifest label those items as unverified and do not claim Gate approval or completed audit.
4. Numerical results come only from registered executed Run/Artifact outputs. Writing roles may explain, narrow or propose claims, but cannot fill missing effects, intervals, sample counts, citations or successful runs. Preserve negative results, failed branches, limitations and evidence conflicts.
5. Missing evidence and unresolved claim checks limit what the report can conclude; they do not block unrelated or requested computation. Invalid parameters, invalid required data structures, authorization failures and unavailable services stop the affected execution with the concrete error. Never describe an unverified finding as verified.
6. Discover actual tools and use the loaded skill's `resourceBase` for included resources. Paths such as `.claude/CLAUDE.md` describe the upstream client's layout and are not executable ZeroWall dependencies. Do not invent missing resource contents. Use the registered research state and installed tools to perform the authorized task, recording any missing supporting resource.
7. Independent writing still follows user-selected review and format decisions. This route does not authorize new transfers, deployment, external publication, or extra model/provider spending.
