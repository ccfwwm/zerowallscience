## 设计令牌
- colors: Backgrounds use warm off-white (#F7EFEA). Primary text and high-contrast accents use dark brown (#713B2C). Secondary blocks and icons alternate between muted terracotta, ochre, and soft peach.
- fonts: Elegant Serif for primary titles and large numerals; clear, legible Sans-Serif for body paragraphs and smaller metadata.
- spacing: Generous margins with 40/60 or 50/50 vertical column splits. Elements have ample breathing room.
- shape_language: A mix of pure circles for icons, slightly rounded rectangles for cards, and organic asymmetrical 'blob' shapes for background accents.
- texture: Flat color fills combined with thin, uniform line-art overlays.
- grid: Primarily 2-column content layouts and 3-to-4 column symmetrical card grids. Center-aligned headers.
- motion_or_depth: Mostly flat design. Occasional subtle drop shadows on floating cards to lift them from the background.
