## 设计令牌
- colors: Base background is warm beige/blush (#EFE3DB), primary text and active accents are deep taupe (#93746D), content containers are pure white (#FFFFFF), secondary data/accents are pale taupe (#DCCBBE).
- fonts: Clean, modern sans-serif. Use varying weights (bold for titles, regular for body). Wide tracking for English subtitles.
- spacing: Generous outer margins. Inner white cards have substantial padding (approx 5-10% of slide height). Content is horizontally distributed with even gutters.
- shape_language: Contrast between organic/fluid (background) and sharp/geometric (foreground cards). Accents use pill shapes and perfect circles.
- texture: Smooth vector with subtle watercolor edge blending on the background abstract shapes. Flat solid colors elsewhere.
- grid: Predominantly centered focal layouts for covers/sections, and symmetrical 3-column or 4-column horizontal splits for content.
- motion_or_depth: Two-layer depth model: flat fluid background layer, overlaid with crisp white rectangular cards. Occasional soft drop shadows on cards.
