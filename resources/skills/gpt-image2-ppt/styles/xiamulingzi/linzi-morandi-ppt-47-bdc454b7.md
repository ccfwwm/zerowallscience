## 设计令牌
- colors: Dusty blue for primary backgrounds and dominant shapes; taupe for secondary accents; white for content containers; dark gray for legible body text.
- fonts: Clean, highly legible sans-serif for both headers and body text, emphasizing clarity over styling.
- spacing: Generous framing margins around the white content container, creating a passe-partout effect against the colored background.
- shape_language: A mix of sharp rectangular containers, pill-shaped tags, and repeating geometric sequences (chevrons, circles).
- texture: Flat graphics with subtle transparency overlaps in the background waves.
- grid: Symmetrical 3-column and 4-column horizontal division grids inside the master white container.
- motion_or_depth: Layered background waves create mild depth, while foreground content remains strictly flat.
