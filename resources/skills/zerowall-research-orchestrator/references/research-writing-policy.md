# ZeroWall Research writing policy 7.0.0-1

Use this route only when the user is writing or reviewing the output of an active ZeroWall ResearchStudy. Ordinary editing, standalone literature reviews and independent manuscript work keep their requested workflow. Determine the active study with `research_study` and actual session context; do not create a clinical study merely to format a document.

For an active study, this policy replaces upstream ARS stage-by-stage confirmation, context-reset, external-client path and default-off claim-audit rules:

1. Read the persistent question, dataset contracts, frozen plan, tasks, evidence and claims. Pass their IDs and this policy to each writing/review role. A Material Passport may describe writing progress but cannot replace ResearchStore or amend the scientific freeze.
2. There are two human scientific gates: approval of the principal question and validation plan before confirmatory execution; approval of final core claims after evidence review. Proceed through authorized drafting, reference checking, formatting and recovery without requesting confirmation after every writing stage. Changes to principal questions, data scope or frozen methods require the relevant study amendment/gate. Missing information that determines the task still requires a concrete question.
3. Core-claim audit is mandatory for a formal research deliverable, irrespective of `ARS_CLAIM_AUDIT` or a downstream optional-audit flag. Load `zerowall-claim-audit`, use actual `research_study.audit_claim` for registered claims, and retain supported, contradicted, unknown and stale evidence distinctly. The model cannot grant itself human approval. A draft may show open issues; formal report generation remains blocked until Host checks and Gate 2 pass.
4. Numerical results come only from registered executed Run/Artifact outputs. Writing roles may explain, narrow or propose claims, but cannot fill missing effects, intervals, sample counts, citations or successful runs. Preserve negative results, failed branches, limitations and evidence conflicts.
5. Missing evidence, unresolved integrity violations and invalid methods stop the affected scientific action. Human approval cannot turn an unsupported result into a verified one. Record the gap and an appropriate amendment or data requirement instead of using an upstream acknowledgement override.
6. Discover actual tools and use the loaded skill's `resourceBase` for included resources. Paths such as `.claude/CLAUDE.md` describe the upstream client's layout and are not executable ZeroWall dependencies. Do not invent missing resource contents. Use the registered research state and installed tools to perform the authorized task, recording any missing supporting resource.
7. Independent writing still follows user-selected review and format decisions. This route does not authorize new transfers, deployment, external publication, or extra model/provider spending.
